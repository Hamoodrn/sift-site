Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sift collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Sift is referred to here as “Sift,” and the people who operate it are referred to as “we” or “us.”

Information we collect

When you create and use a Sift account, we collect:

  • Your email address, used for authentication and to identify your account
  • Your name
  • Your self-selected age range
  • Authentication and provider identity information, such as the method you use to sign in
  • Account and profile timestamps and identifiers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and backend infrastructure. As with most online services, standard infrastructure metadata — such as IP address, network, and request information — may be processed by our infrastructure service providers as part of operating the service.

How you sign in

Sift supports email and password authentication and may also offer authentication through third-party providers such as Google and Apple. When you use a third-party sign-in method, that provider and our authentication infrastructure process information needed to authenticate your account.

How news summaries are generated

Sift uses OpenAI, server-side, to process news source content and generate the summaries you read in the app. Your profile or account personal data is not used to generate or personalize news summaries.

Every summary links back to its original source articles. Following those links takes you to external websites, which have their own privacy practices that we do not control.

What Sift does not currently collect

Sift does not currently collect:

  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Camera data
  • Microphone or audio data
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Health data
  • Product analytics
  • Third-party tracking
  • Personalized advertising
  • In-app browsing history

Data retention

We retain your account and profile data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your Sift account at any time from Account. This permanently removes your account and profile data and cannot be undone.

Short-lived infrastructure backups or logs may persist for a limited time after deletion where technically necessary, for example as part of routine backup cycles, before being cleared in the ordinary course of operating our infrastructure.

Age

Sift asks users to select an age range as part of their profile. We do not currently use age range for advertising, targeting, or personalization. Sift is not designed specifically for children.

Security

We use encrypted transport and access controls to help protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We do not hold any particular security certification.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about your data, you can reach us at siftnews.support@gmail.com.

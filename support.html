Sift Support

If you have a question, run into a problem, or want to report an issue with the Sift app, contact us at siftnews.support@gmail.com.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app: go to Sift → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and profile data.

Account and data questions

For any other account or data questions, including questions about what information Sift stores, you can reach us at the same support address: siftnews.support@gmail.com.
